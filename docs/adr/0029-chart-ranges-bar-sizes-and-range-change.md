# Chart ranges, bar sizes and range change

The detail chart offers six ranges, each with a fixed bar size: 1D at 5 minutes (today's session on a full-session axis, with a dashed line at the previous close), 1W at 30 minutes, 1M at 1 hour, 3M and 1Y at 1 day, and 5Y at 1 week. The detail header's change follows the selected range, as on Robinhood and Google Finance ("Today", "Past year"), while the list always shows today's change. Chart colour follows the range's direction and is always paired with a sign and an arrow, never colour alone. Scrubbing shows the price, the time in ET, and the change from the start of the range to that point, computed with the shared money module (ADR 0015).
